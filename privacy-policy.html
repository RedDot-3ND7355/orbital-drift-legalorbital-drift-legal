Privacy Policy for Orbital Drift

Effective Date: November 23, 2026  |  Developer: Kogaruh

1. Overview

Kogaruh ("we," "our," or "us") developed Orbital Drift as a commercial mobile game for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play Orbital Drift.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

We do not operate custom external servers to collect, store, or sell your personal data. However, Orbital Drift uses local device storage and optional Google Play Services features to provide gameplay functionality:

  • Local Game Progress & Preferences: Your in-game progress, high scores, sector records, unlocked suits, boots, grav-boards, contract completion status, and audio/control settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Google Play Games Services (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games Services, Google processes your Play Games Player ID, gamer tag/display name, avatar, leaderboard scores, and achievement progress in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy so you can appear on public leaderboards and track achievements.
  • Google Play Billing & Diagnostics: App purchases and standard crash or performance diagnostics provided through Google Play are processed by Google LLC under Google’s terms and privacy policies.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties, and Orbital Drift does not serve third-party behavioral advertisements. Information processed by Google Play Services is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Device Data: Game progress stored locally on your device remains there until you clear the app’s data in Android Settings or uninstall Orbital Drift.
  • Google Play Games Data: You may manage or delete your Play Games profile and associated game activity at any time via your Google Play Games app settings or Google Account data controls.

5. Children’s Privacy

Orbital Drift is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at [Your Support Email Address] so we can address it promptly.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
